SCENE: a wall in a dim studio covered with white and off-white index cards and torn paper notes pinned in loose clusters, connected by taut red and lime-green threads between metal pins. The cards carry only quick abstract pencil strokes, arrows, boxes and circles, with no legible words. A single warm work light from the top of the frame rakes across the wall, casting long soft shadows from the pins and curled paper edges. Seen slightly from the side, the wall recedes into darkness toward the right of the frame.
