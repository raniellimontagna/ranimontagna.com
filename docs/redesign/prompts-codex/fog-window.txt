SCENE: early morning inside a farmhouse kitchen in the Serra Gaúcha, southern Brazil. An open laptop sits on a worn wooden table near a tall wooden-framed window; its screen shows a dark abstract interface with a single lime-green highlight and no legible text. Outside, thick white fog fills a valley of green hills and araucaria pine trees with their distinctive umbrella-shaped crowns. Soft diffused daylight spills in and wraps the edge of the table, a ceramic coffee cup beside the laptop, the rest of the room falls into deep shadow.
